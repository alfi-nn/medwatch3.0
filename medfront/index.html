Innovation

Transforming Drug Safety with Predictive Intelligence

Traditional pharmacovigilance is reactive — adverse effects are discovered after patients are harmed. MedAI Insights shifts the paradigm to proactive prediction, combining deep learning models with explainable AI to flag high-risk drug-patient interactions before prescriptions are written.
